Selected Projects

HyperMamba: Industrial Lens Defect Detection Based on Hypergraph-Enhanced Mamba Vision

  • I designed an industrial defect detection algorithm based on a Mamba-Vision backbone and hypergraph modeling.
  • The model achieved 97.10% AP50, 98.48% AP20, 64.68 FPS, and improved 8.5% AP50 over the baseline MambaVision.

DeepVFR: Identity-Preserving Video Face Retouching Dataset and Benchmark

  • I proposed a large-scale paired benchmark for video face retouching through an image-retouching-pair to image-to-video to face-swapping pipeline.
  • The benchmark contains 1,024 temporally aligned video pairs and 153,600 paired frames.

Diffusion-Mamba Face Retouching Scheme

  • This project uses open datasets from Alibaba, Megvii, and Tencent, combining diffusion-based retouching and Mamba super-resolution for face enhancement.
  • The project received funding support from Shenzhen Haokeda (RMB 400,000), the National Undergraduate Innovation Program (RMB 20,000), and other regional and university programs.

Multimodal Teacher Quality Assessment System

  • I led a national undergraduate innovation project on multimodal posture recognition for teaching-quality evaluation.
  • The project proposes an optimized YOLO-based model with a classification-guided frame-skipping strategy to reduce computational cost for long videos.

QClaw Authorized Penetration Testing and Compliance Evaluation System

  • I participated in the design of a four-layer architecture including web presentation, orchestration, multi-agent scanning, and authorized cyber range.
  • The system supports structured security intent recognition, toolchain orchestration, and automated compliance-oriented evaluation.
